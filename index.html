01 — the foundation

Why balance matters

Your body is built from trillions of individually living cells — and every one of them depends on the fats you eat to build its outer wall.

Every cell in your body is wrapped in a membrane built largely from fatty acids. That membrane isn't just a wall — it's where cells send and receive signals, regulate what enters and exits, and communicate with everything around them. The composition of that membrane depends directly on the fats you consume.

Modern diets have shifted dramatically over the last century — richer in omega-6 fats from refined oils, lighter in omega-3s from fish and whole foods. Researchers describe this as a change in the omega-6-to-omega-3 ratio, and it's one of the more significant nutritional shifts in human history.
